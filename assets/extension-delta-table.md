Assess the additions as a coherent scientific argument. More pages, more prose, renamed components, and a longer reference list do not by themselves explain a new contribution. Do not assert that any fixed percentage guarantees compliance or acceptance.

Reusable disclosure structure, to be completed with verified facts:

> A preliminary version of this work appeared in [conference citation]. The present article extends that study by [addition and scientific purpose], [addition and scientific purpose], and [new evidence]. The original [method/result] is retained as [role], while Sections [locations] present the new contributions.
